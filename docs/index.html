BNB Hack · Tokenized Stocks Edition

Tokenized stocks trade all weekend.
Their reference price doesn't.

From Friday 16:00 ET to Monday 09:30 ET the stock market prints nothing, but Ondo, xStocks and bStocks tokens keep trading on BNB Chain. A stop-loss cannot fill at a price the market never prints, so an agent that sizes weekend trades like weekday trades is taking a risk it cannot see. Weekend Gap Guard is the pre-trade gate in front of the wallet: every order comes back ALLOW REDUCE or BLOCK, with the reason.

How it works

    
    
      
  1. Data integrityNo price, a quote older than 15 min, a pool that has not traded for 24 h, or no reference while the market is shut → BLOCK. Fail closed.
    2. 
      
  2. Venue statusASSET_PAUSED / MARKET_PAUSED / maintenance → BLOCK. Earnings-limited → gap ×2.5, size ×0.5.
    3. 
      
  3. Session phaseopen · pre-close (last hour) · closed · pre-open (last 2 h). Holiday weekends cut deeper.
    4. 
      
  4. Risk per dollarWhile the stock trades: the stop. While it is shut: the 99 % weekend-gap quantile, stretched by any upward drift you buy into. ×1.43 for the worst fill we have seen.
    5. 
      
  5. Stale referenceWeekend drift vs Friday close (in σ of a weekend move); distance from the cross-issuer median; premium to a live stock print.
    6. 
      
  6. CapsPosition cap by phase, gross-exposure ("leverage") cap, issuer notional limit, and account guards: static floor, daily loss limit, 40 % consistency rule.
    7. 
      
  7. Verdictallowed = min(request, caps). Exits are never sized down.
    8.

What is real and what is not

      
    
        
  • Prices, candles, statuses and issuer limits come from the public Binance Wallet Skills endpoints (no key). Balances come from public BSC JSON-RPC. All replays use recorded hourly candles; the live board uses our own recordings.
    • 
        
  • The guard does not trade. The Agentic Wallet hand-off prints the baw commands; we could not run them because the Agentic Wallet requires a Binance account and the Binance app.
    • 
        
  • Historical venue status (pauses, earnings) is not available from the endpoints, so replays mark it "unknown"; the live path reads it.
    • 
        
  • The reference close is the Ondo token's 16:00 ET hourly close (Ondo tracks the stock within ~0.1 % in regular hours), not an exchange feed.
